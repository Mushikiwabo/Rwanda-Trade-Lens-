    INTERPRETATION

    Coverage matters

    • Each month includes ranked countries, “Rest of the World,” and a reported total.
    • Countries not listed in a monthly top-ten snapshot are unknown for that period—not zero.
    • Some later month blocks have no year label in the workbook. They are kept as “Year unspecified.”
    • No product-by-destination cross-tab is provided, so category and destination filters cannot be combined into a joint result.
